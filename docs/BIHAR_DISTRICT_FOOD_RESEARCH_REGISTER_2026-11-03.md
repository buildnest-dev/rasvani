# Bihar District Food Research Register

**Research started:** 3 November 2026  
**Scope:** Traditional vegetarian food discovery for all 38 Bihar districts.  
**Publication rule:** This is an editorial research register, not a published catalogue. A dish is not automatically district-specific because it is eaten across Bihar. Do not publish an origin claim without stronger evidence. Exact-dish images and product availability are separate approval gates.

2. Government of Bihar, Tourism Department, “Magnificent Bihar” cuisine brochure: https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/content/dam/bihar-tourism/e_brochures/magnificient_bihar/magnificent_bihar_english_2017.pdf — independently corroborates Maner laddoo, Udwantnagar belgrami, Silao khaja, and Gaya tilkut/anarsa.

4. Government of Bihar, Tourism Department, “Khichdi”: https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/en/experiences/food-and-cuisine/main-course/khichdi — supports khichdi as a widely consumed Bihar food and festival preparation, not a unique district association.
