# Phase 4 — Regional catalogue filters

## Implemented

The public Shop filter form now supports four dimensions: free-text product search, state/Union Territory, approved district and active category. The region selector is populated from the canonical 36-region application taxonomy. District options are loaded only from district records whose publication status is approved.

Regional and district catalogue scoping is based on `product_regional_associations` records with `review_status = approved`. District-specific filtering resolves the selected approved district record first and then limits products to its association records. Region filtering includes approved associations attached to the selected region. The regional scope is re-applied after alias matches are merged so a reviewed spelling cannot bypass the selected region or district.

The existing product publication checks remain in force. Category, text search and regional filters can be combined. Invalid region/district combinations return no products rather than silently falling back to the unfiltered catalogue. Clearing filters resets all dimensions.

## Guardrails

- No food or regional relationship is generated by the filter UI.
- A region or district association is not an exclusive-origin claim unless the separately reviewed association type and evidence support that wording.
- Product price, stock, delivery coverage, ETA and checkout remain unavailable until the corresponding commercial and fulfilment decisions and integrations are approved.
- The district selector is disabled until a valid region is selected.
- This increment does not introduce an interactive map or make any map-provider/licensing decision.

## Verification

The static QA suite checks the route wiring, approved-record filters, alias-scope reapplication, invalid-filter behavior, preserved category/search controls and responsive CSS. It does not execute Supabase queries or replace live database, browser, typecheck, build, security, accessibility or performance testing.
