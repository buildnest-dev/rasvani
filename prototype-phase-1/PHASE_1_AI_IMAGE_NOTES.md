# Phase 1 AI image notes

AI-generated food imagery is used only as illustrative design content in this prototype. It is not evidence of an actual product, producer, place of origin, packaging, licence, certification or supply relationship.

The current local asset set includes hero, Bihar, West Bengal, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu food visuals, collection visuals and story visuals. Browser QA confirmed all 10 referenced assets loaded successfully at desktop, tablet and mobile test sizes.

Important limitation: the first image generation pass returned full webpage mockups rather than standalone photographs. Some image regions were extracted from those mockups. This is a visual placeholder compromise, not a final production photography library. Before launch, replace these with authentic, rights-cleared photographs that accurately depict the relevant regional foods and do not misrepresent producers or products.
