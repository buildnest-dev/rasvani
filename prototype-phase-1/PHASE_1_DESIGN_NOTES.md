# Phase 1 design notes — verified prototype

The prototype uses the Rasvani heritage-meets-modern direction: warm cream, deep maroon, terracotta, forest green and harvest gold; editorial serif headings; responsive layouts; regional discovery; product preview; Better Choices; food stories; retailer and producer journeys; and a future admin workspace preview.

The homepage copy follows the governing PDF's emotional message: “Some tastes are not just food. They're home.” and its supporting promise to discover authentic traditional foods from India's states and districts.

All 36 state/UT names are present in the selector. Bihar is the first editorial focus. The other regional cards are previews only; no unverified region/product claims are published.

The prototype contains no live account, checkout, payment, producer application, inventory, warehouse, B2B enquiry or CMS backend. Each is clearly presented as a preview or future capability.

Browser QA screenshots and test results are included alongside this file. See `PHASE_1_VERIFICATION_REPORT.md`.
